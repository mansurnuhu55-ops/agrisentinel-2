How to delete your account

You can delete your AgriSentinel AI account and your data yourself, at any time.

Option 1: inside the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. On the Dashboard, scroll to your account card.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete everything.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone, email and sign-in.
  • Farmers: all your animals and cases, and the vet advice on them.
  • Veterinary surgeons: your profile. Advice you wrote on a farmer's case stays on that farmer's record but is no longer linked to you.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. A copy of the app's sign-in on your phone is cleared when you sign out.
