Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. AgriSentinel AI is an early prototype for livestock farmers and veterinary surgeons in Nigeria.

Who runs this app

Dr. Mansur Nuhu. Phone / WhatsApp: 08062246974. Email: mansurnuhu55@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number, email address and whether you are a farmer or a veterinary surgeon.
  • For veterinary surgeons: your VCN (registration number), used to approve your account.
  • Your animals: type, tag, age, sex, location you type in, and the owner name and phone.
  • Your case reports: signs, duration, how many animals are affected, notes, status and history.
  • Advice and notes written by a veterinary surgeon on a case.

We do not collect your GPS location, contacts, photos, or payment details. We use no advertising and no tracking or analytics tools.

Why we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, keep your animal and case records, and let a farmer and a veterinary surgeon communicate about a case.

Who can see your information

  • Farmers: you see only your own animals and cases.
  • Approved veterinary surgeons: can see cases that have not yet been taken by a vet, including the animal details and the owner's name and phone, so they can respond. Once a vet takes a case, other vets no longer see it.
  • The administrator (the app builder) can access the database to approve vets and maintain the service.
  • We never sell your information.

Where it is stored

Your account and records are stored with Supabase (data centre in Ireland, EU). Passwords are handled by Supabase sign-in and are stored in scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot read them. The app pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser also keeps a sign-in session on your phone until you sign out.

Sharing on WhatsApp

If you tap a share button, the case summary is sent through WhatsApp by you. We do not send anything to WhatsApp on our own.

Deleting your information

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open the Dashboard, scroll to your account card and tap Delete my account. This removes your profile and, for farmers, all your animals and cases. For veterinary surgeons, advice already written on a farmer's case stays on that farmer's record but is no longer linked to your account. If you cannot sign in, contact us using the details above and we will delete your data within 7 days. See also How to delete your account.

Children

This app is for adults aged 18 and over.

Important safety notice

AgriSentinel AI is an information tool. It does not diagnose disease, prescribe treatment or give drug doses, and it does not replace a visit by a veterinary surgeon. In an emergency call a veterinary surgeon immediately. Suggestions shown to veterinary surgeons are only prompts to support their own professional judgement.

Your rights and the law

We aim to handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You may ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will appear on this page.
